# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who want their current calendar and important dates visible on an iPhone lock screen. They manage the wallpaper from a lightweight web dashboard or a private Telegram conversation, often from a phone and in short bursts.

## Product Purpose

Calendar Lockscreen turns calendar ranges, truthful period progress, and upcoming-event countdowns into a live iPhone wallpaper. Success means users can understand their current preset, range, presentation, and lock-screen fit, make a change quickly, and trust that the dashboard, Telegram bot, calendar data, and next wallpaper refresh stay in sync.

Living decorations are deliberately static deterministic frames, not a weather feed or live animation.

## Brand Personality

Clean, premium, and calm. The product should feel precise and quietly helpful rather than playful, chatty, or ornamental.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy chatbot prose, decorative emoji overload, long unstructured status dumps, exposed implementation details, and interfaces that make routine actions feel complicated.

## Design Principles

- Put the next useful action close to the information that motivates it.
- Prefer concise, scannable summaries over prose-heavy explanations.
- Keep commands and outcomes consistent across Telegram and the dashboard.
- Separate setup or recovery details from everyday use.
- Make synchronization state and delayed wallpaper updates explicit without repeating them everywhere.
- Treat lock-screen spacing as user-calibrated: reserve conservative clock/widget and notification regions, but never claim one pixel-safe layout fits every device.
- Keep content anchors stable as event counts change, and reveal overflow rather than compressing text into unsafe regions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use plain language, meaningful labels, and native platform controls. Do not rely on color, formatting, or emoji alone to communicate state. Keep message structures readable by screen readers and resilient when Telegram formatting is unavailable.
